Drone view of waves crashing against rugged cliffs along Big Sur’s Garay Point beach. Blue waters create white-tipped waves as golden sunset light illuminates the rocky shore. A small island with a lighthouse sits in the distance, and green shrubbery covers the cliff’s edge. The steep drop from the road down to the beach is a dramatic feat, with cliff edges jutting dramatically over the sea. This captures the raw beauty and rugged landscape of the Pacific Coast Highway.
